There has been a model launch almost every day this week — GPT-6 Sol and Luna, Claude Opus 5.5 and Google's new text-to-speech models. But the case that held my attention is not a recent launch. It is the way Stripe chose to automate work inside the company first.

On July 30, Stripe introduced its Knowledge AI Platform. According to the company, the agent is already connected to more than a thousand internal tools and systems. It handles everything from a simple question to projects that would take days of manual work.

## In brief

- The Knowledge AI Platform connects an agent to more than a thousand tools and systems at Stripe.
- The company began by automating internal work before considering exposing the platform to customers.
- Security and compliance were part of the design from the start.
- For teams building products with sensitive data, productivity needs to come with governance, traceability and trust.

## The choice to start inside the company

What caught my attention was not only the scale. It was the order of the decisions: build for internal use before considering the same capability for customers.

Automating knowledge work with real security and compliance is not trivial, especially at a company that handles money all day. Stripe's [technical account](https://stripe.dev/blog/meet-stripes-knowledge-ai-platform) offers a closer look at how the platform was built.

This connects directly with the work we do on products tied to credit and receivables. How far should we automate with AI without losing control of the process? Good automation is not just about speed. It also needs [AI governance](/en/guides/ai-governance/), traceability and the trust of the people who use the system every day.

## What to consider before expanding autonomy

I work in product, thinking about how AI can simplify structured credit and receivables processes without becoming a black box. Cases like this are useful benchmarks: they show that real productivity gains can go hand in hand with control over what an agent can and cannot do.

For product teams, this calls for clear decisions about the process being automated, the systems involved and the limits of the agent's autonomy. An [AI agent](/en/guides/ai-agents/) with access to many tools also needs supervision criteria and a way to assess risk before reaching a customer journey. It is an [AI product management](/en/guides/ai-product-management/) discussion that can be organized with an [AI risk matrix](/en/guides/ai-risk-matrix/).

The future of finance will be a mix of people and AI agents working side by side, each doing what they do best. Those who learn early how to balance autonomy and safety will build better products.
