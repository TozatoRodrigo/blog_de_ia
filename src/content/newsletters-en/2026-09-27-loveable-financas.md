This week’s radar brought near-simultaneous launches from four major AI labs and a new agent tool from NVIDIA. But the story that caught my attention was smaller: a generative AI builder aimed specifically at finance. That shift toward vertical AI builders is the thread I chose to explore today.

Every week, a new tool sells itself as the “Loveable for something.” This week, it was finance.

I saw a story about Ekselio, which describes itself as an AI-powered financial app generator with a *local-first* approach. In practice, you describe the workflow you need, and the tool builds an application around it.

## Why AI builders are moving into specific industries

What caught my attention was not the tool itself, but the shift behind it. General-purpose AI builders have already shown that they can generate code and interfaces quickly. Now the race is on to bring those capabilities to specific niches, and finance is one of the first sectors to get a dedicated tool.

## What this changes for financial product teams

This changes the game for people working on financial operations products, whether in credit, receivables, payments, or process automation. Prototyping a workflow, validating a screen with the business team, and testing a hypothesis before bringing in engineering all get faster when the tool already understands the industry’s vocabulary and rules.

From a product perspective, this does not replace the part that matters most: understanding the right problem to solve. That remains a [product management with AI](/en/guides/ai-product-management/) decision. But a specialized builder can reduce the friction between having an idea and putting something in front of users to test.

## Security and sensitive data belong in the design

Finance has specific security, compliance, and sensitive-data requirements that any tool like this needs to account for from the start, not later. Evaluating those limits is also part of [AI governance](/en/guides/ai-governance/) — especially when a team decides which workflows and data it can bring into a new tool.

I’m optimistic about this kind of shift. The more AI understands the context of each industry, the easier it becomes for product teams to test real ideas with less dependence on long development cycles.

If you’re curious and want to learn more about the tool, here is the link: [Ekselio](https://www.gptbeyond.com/try?home=1).
